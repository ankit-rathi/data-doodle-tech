# Publishing checklist

## Editorial and scripts
- [ ] One question and learning outcome
- [ ] Claims checked; sources/caveats recorded
- [ ] Hook creates a reason to watch
- [ ] EN/DE/HI meaning aligned and natural
- [ ] Final audio timed; no rushed delivery or cut sentences

## Visual and audio
- [ ] 9:16 vertical; safe area respected
- [ ] Minimal, legible labels; correct arrows/relationships
- [ ] No overlap, clipping, occlusion or visual contradiction
- [ ] Animation follows semantic audio beats
- [ ] Speech clear; music not competing
- [ ] Captions checked; phone-size review completed
- [ ] Final export watched/listened to end-to-end

## YouTube
- [ ] Searchable title, main phrase early
- [ ] Unique description; first sentence states topic
- [ ] 3–5 relevant hashtags
- [ ] Correct playlist and readable cover frame
- [ ] Useful pinned comment

## Instagram
- [ ] Hook-first caption
- [ ] Caption adds value; not just transcript dump
- [ ] 3–5 relevant hashtags
- [ ] One CTA and accurate alt text
- [ ] Cover legible in profile grid; Story share considered

## After publishing
- [ ] Save URL and publish date
- [ ] Capture 48-hour and 7-day metrics
- [ ] Record audience questions and sequel ideas
