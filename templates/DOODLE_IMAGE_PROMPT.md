# Reusable doodle image prompt

Create one high-quality vertical 9:16 illustration for Data Doodle Tech.

**Topic:** [TOPIC]  
**Core concept:** [ONE SENTENCE]  
**Entities:** [OBJECTS/ACTORS]  
**Relationships:** [FLOWS/ARROWS]  
**Metaphor:** [METAPHOR]  
**Essential labels (3–7):** [LABELS]  
**Limited palette:** [PALETTE]

Modern hand-drawn whiteboard/doodle illustration with confident imperfect marker strokes. Friendly, intelligent, playful but technically credible. Clean light background, strong dark outlines, restrained accent palette, clear hierarchy and generous whitespace. One coherent composition, not unrelated panels. Use simple characters, objects, arrows and containers only when they clarify the idea. Make flow direction unmistakable. Large legible labels, central safe area, phone-first readability. Artwork should communicate the idea muted and remain reusable for English, German and Hindi narration.

No paragraphs or full narration in the image; no tiny text, clutter, random icons, stock imagery, photorealism, 3D, watermark or signature. Do not invent technical relationships.
