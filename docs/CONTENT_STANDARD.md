# Content publication standard

## Editorial promise
Each episode makes one technical idea easier to understand. A viewer should be able to repeat its central mental model after one watch.

## Topic selection
Prefer topics with search intent, practical/interview value, strong visual metaphor, sequel potential and a scope that can be explained accurately in 30–60 seconds. If too complex, split into a series.

## Research
Use primary documentation, standards or authoritative technical sources. Record URLs and access date in `research.md`. List facts, simplifications and caveats. Distinguish common practice from universal law. Never invent benchmarks or capabilities.

## Script structure
Default target: roughly 115–135 English words for 50–60 seconds, but actual audio timing wins.
1. Hook (0–3s)
2. Topic framing (3–7s)
3. Explanation in 2–4 beats (7–40s)
4. Mental model/payoff (40–53s)
5. Takeaway and at most one CTA (final seconds)

Write for speech: short sentences, active voice, no long intro. Explain acronyms where practical. German and Hindi must be idiomatic and technically accurate. Keep common technical acronyms where practitioners use them. Do not split sentences awkwardly across animation beats.

## Visual standard
- 9:16, ideally 1080×1920; central safe area.
- One coherent composition and visual metaphor.
- Hierarchy: central concept > relationships/arrows > short labels > decoration.
- Prefer 3–7 short labels; never print the whole narration.
- Modern hand-drawn marker style, limited palette, clear contrast, generous whitespace.
- Arrows must be unambiguous; no false causal relationships.
- Language-neutral artwork; minimal English labels where needed.

## Audio and animation
Narration is the timing master for each language. Finish audio before precise animation timing. Align changes with semantic beats; preserve sentence boundaries and breathing room. Check acronym pronunciation. If language tracks differ in duration, adjust timing independently.

## QA
Verify facts and caveats; spelling; label legibility; arrow direction; overlap/clipping/occlusion; mobile-size readability; narration pronunciation; subtitle accuracy; sync; correct 9:16 export. Watch and listen end-to-end.

## YouTube Shorts packaging
Searchable title with main phrase early. First description sentence says what the video explains. Add useful context, related terms, one CTA, 3–5 relevant hashtags as a starting convention, and the right playlist. Use a readable cover frame.

## Instagram Reels packaging
First caption line = hook/topic. Add 2–4 short paragraphs with natural keywords. Use one save/share/follow CTA, 3–5 relevant hashtags as a starting convention, and accurate alt text.

## Measurement
Record publish date, platform, title, hook, duration, views/reach, average view duration, average percentage viewed, viewed-vs-swiped where available, shares, saves, comments, profile visits and follows/subscribers. Review at 48 hours and 7 days.
