# English

There are no perfect solutions in data systems architecture—only trade-offs.

Imagine you're designing a system. You want high performance, but that may cost more. You want to keep costs low, but that can limit performance. And adding more features can make the system harder to operate.

So the goal isn't to maximize everything.

Instead, you choose the right balance for your application—based on its workload, scale, reliability needs, budget and future growth.

Good architecture isn't about finding a perfect system.

It's about finding the right trade-off.
