# Research notes

This is a general engineering principle, not a quantitative claim.

- Architecture choices involve competing quality attributes and constraints.
- Improving one attribute can impose costs or constraints elsewhere, depending on workload and design.
- Appropriate choices depend on workload, scale, reliability, budget, team capability and expected change.

**Caveat:** Do not imply every performance improvement necessarily increases cost or complexity; relationships are context-dependent.

Suggested reading: Martin Kleppmann, *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*; AWS Well-Architected Framework https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/welcome.html ; Google Cloud Architecture Framework https://cloud.google.com/architecture/framework .
