**Opening:** There's no perfect data system. Here's why. 👇

Every architecture decision involves trade-offs.

More performance may require more resources. Lower cost may constrain capacity. More features can increase operational complexity.

The right design depends on the workload, scale, reliability needs, budget and what the system may need to do next.

**Good architecture isn't about maximizing everything. It's about choosing the right balance.** ✏️
