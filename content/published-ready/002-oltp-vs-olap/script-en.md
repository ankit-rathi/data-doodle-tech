# English

Your app and your analytics team may use data—but they need very different kinds of systems.

Operational systems, or OLTP, handle everyday transactions: placing an order, making a payment or updating a customer record. They are designed for lots of fast, reliable operations.

Analytical systems, or OLAP, answer questions across larger datasets. They help teams aggregate information, explore history and spot trends.

Data is often prepared and moved between these worlds using ETL: Extract, Transform and Load.

A data warehouse typically stores curated data for analysis, while a data lake can hold a wider variety of data formats.

The simple way to remember it?

OLTP runs the business. OLAP helps you understand it.
