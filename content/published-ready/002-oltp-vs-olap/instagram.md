**Opening:** Your app database and your analytics database have different jobs. Here's why. 👇

**OLTP** handles everyday transactions—orders, payments and customer updates.

**OLAP** helps analyze larger datasets, historical information and business trends.

And **ETL**—Extract, Transform and Load—is one way to prepare and move data from operational systems into analytical platforms.

Remember it this way: **OLTP runs the business. OLAP helps you understand it.** ✏️

Save this for your next data engineering interview.
